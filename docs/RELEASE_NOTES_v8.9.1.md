# Fizgig v8.9.1: maintenance

- **Previews that stay on your GPU say so.** A helper renders a normal LoRA run's previews. For a slider, an edit LoRA, a fine-tune, or a run previewing on a separate model (such as Klein's Distilled), the previews render on your GPU, and the Samples tab, the helper's Previews tile and the log now say so, instead of the Samples tab saying the helper renders them.
- **The helpers guide's "Not yet" list is in plain words:** which previews stay on your GPU, one helper per training share, and which runs can't move yet. [Helpers](https://github.com/shootthesound/Fizgig/blob/master/docs/HELPERS.md#not-yet)

Everything new in 8.9: [Fizgig v8.9.0 release notes](https://github.com/shootthesound/Fizgig/releases/tag/v8.9.0).

**Updating:** update Fizgig on your computers (helpers need the same version), and restart your pods.
